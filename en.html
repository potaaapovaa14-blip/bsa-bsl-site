About Us

A young company with a clear focus

BSA LLC is registered in Krasnodar Krai, near Novorossiysk — Russia's largest grain port. We bring sourcing, storage, logistics and export together into one controlled cycle, so every shipment reaches the buyer on time and at the declared quality.

We are focused on long-term partnerships with traders, grain elevators and shipping operators, and are open to cooperation in both domestic and export markets.
